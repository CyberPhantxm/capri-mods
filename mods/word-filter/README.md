# Word Filter

Stops her saying words you don't want, in both her typed replies and what she says out loud.

Out of the box it removes **choom** (and *chewm*, how her voice spells it) and any sentence with **good boy** or **good girl**. Add your own words or phrases, comma separated, in Options. Removing a word tidies up around it: "Nice try, choom!" becomes "Nice try!", and "Choom, you good?" becomes "You good?". A line that was only banned words becomes "Nice."

**Options:** remove choom · remove good boy/girl · your own words

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Quaisar_THM
- Needs: Capri with mod API 3 (0.8.7 or newer), Windows
- Permissions: chat, voice

### Why the mod check flags it
- **Changes what her brain is told**: it edits her replies to take the banned words out.

No network access, no keys, nothing sent anywhere.
